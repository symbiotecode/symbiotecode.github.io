## What Is Varnex?

[Varnex](https://varnex.xyz/) is a freemium VPS hosting service. Instead of paying upfront, you can earn credits by watching ads, or you can pay monthly for a plan. That makes it an easy way to get a small virtual server for little or no money.

## How Varnex Works

1. **Earn credits.** Watch ads to earn credits, or pay monthly instead.
2. **Select your plan.** Pick one of the plans below.
3. **Redeem your credits.** Redeem them to get your VPS.

Varnex is a decent freemium option for hosting webpages, testing code, sandboxing, and learning Linux. The resources are small, so it's not a good fit for game servers or a VPN. If you want a free VPS to practice on, it's worth a try.
